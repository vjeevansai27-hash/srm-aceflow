⚠️ Faculty Requirement Protection: Submitted worksheet links must be stored in and open from your own personal Google Drive. Synthetic/fake links are never allowed. Every link submitted opens genuinely on Google Drive!
✦ Method 1: Personal Drive Folder Link (Fastest — 10 Seconds) 10 Secs

1. Open drive.google.com and open your folder (e.g. srmworksheets).
2. Right-click folder > Share > Anyone with the link can view > Copy link.
3. Paste folder link below. When solving, AceFlow downloads the solved PDF and opens your folder tab so you can drop the file in!

✦ Method 2: 100% Automated Background Uploader (Zero Click)

Want AceFlow to upload PDFs directly inside your Drive folder automatically without drag & drop?
1. Open script.google.com > Click New project.
2. Delete default text, paste copied script, click Deploy > New deployment.
3. Select type Web app, set Who has access to Anyone, and click Deploy.
💡 If prompted with "Google hasn't verified this app", click Advanced > Go to Untitled project (unsafe) > Allow.
4. Paste your Web App URL below and click Test & Connect!
